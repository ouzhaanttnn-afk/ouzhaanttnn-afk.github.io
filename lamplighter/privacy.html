Privacy Policy

Last updated: 15 November 2026

Lamplighter is a game made by Lumoza. It does not collect, store or share any personal data.

  • No accounts. You never sign up or log in.
  • No ads and no tracking. The game contains no advertising, analytics or tracking code of any kind.
  • Nothing leaves your phone. Your progress and settings are saved only on your device. The game has no servers and sends nothing over the internet.
  • Purchases are handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. We never see your name, payment details or Apple account. The game only learns which items you own, so it can unlock them.
  • Children. Because no data is collected, the game is safe for players of every age.

If you delete the game, your saved progress is deleted with it. Purchases can be restored at any time from the game's Shop or Settings screen.

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be posted on this page. Questions: see the support page.
