Privacy Policy

App: Ebru Atelier: Color Puzzle (on device “Ebru: Color Atelier”) · Last updated: November 8, 2026

In short: We don't ask you to create an account, we don't ask for your name or email, and we run no servers. Your progress stays on your device. To keep the game free we use Google's ad service (AdMob), which collects some data from your device. Personalized ads require your permission, and you can change that choice in Settings at any time.

1. Who we are

Ouzhan Alper Tosun, independent developer, Türkiye. Contact: studionostos.dev@gmail.com

2. Data that stays on your device (never sent to us)

  • Game progress: unlocked levels, stars, coins, purchased styles, gallery.
  • Settings: language, sound, music, vibration, accessibility options, your ad consent choice.
  • Error log: if the game hits an error, a short summary of the last 20 errors (time, error type, code line). It contains no personal data and is only included in an email if you tap “Send Feedback”; you can see and delete it before sending.

This data is removed when you delete the app.

3. Data collected by our ad partner (Google AdMob)

The game shows banner, interstitial and optional rewarded ads. Google's ad software may collect:

Google processes this data under its own policy: policies.google.com/privacy · How Google uses data from partner apps: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

4. Your choices

  • EEA, UK and Switzerland: Google's consent form appears on first launch. Change your choice in Settings → “Privacy Options”.
  • Türkiye (KVKK): On first launch we ask for your explicit consent to personalized ads and transfer of data abroad. If you decline you only see non-personalized ads; the game works the same. Change it in Settings → “Personalized ads consent”.
  • iOS (App Tracking Transparency): Apple's permission prompt asks before the advertising ID is used. Change it in iPhone Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • Android: Reset or delete your Advertising ID in Settings → Privacy → Ads.

5. Legal bases and transfers

Personalized advertising and the related international transfer to Google LLC (USA) rely on your consent. Showing non-personalized ads to keep the game free relies on our legitimate interest. You have the right to access, correct, delete, object and withdraw consent; contact studionostos.dev@gmail.com and we will reply within 30 days. You can also use Google's tools at myadcenter.google.com.

6. Purchases

“Remove Ads” is bought entirely through the Apple App Store or Google Play. We never see your payment details; the app only checks with the store whether the purchase is valid and remembers it on your device.

7. Game services

If leaderboards and achievements are enabled, your scores are sent to Apple Game Center or Google Play Games under your own account and their terms.

8. Children

The game is intended for ages 13 and up. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13.

9. Security and retention

We run no servers, so we store no data about you. Our ad partner's data is encrypted in transit; retention follows Google's policy.

10. Changes and contact

If we update this policy, the date above changes. Questions: studionostos.dev@gmail.com
